Mon histoire

Learning feels easier with a friend.

Learn French with Vineet Bro is a French learning platform built around a simple idea: learning becomes easier when it feels like a conversation with a friend over a cup of coffee.

I’m a French and English language trainer, educator and bilingual translator with over 15 years of experience. As founder and lead trainer, I help learners build confidence and communicate naturally in French.

Having worked as a bilingual translator with multinational companies, I bring real-world professional communication into every lesson. My approach is simple: let’s learn, practise, and get everything sorted out over a cup of coffee.
